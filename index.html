Examination Rules & Terms

Please read carefully before proceeding.

Mandatory Full-Screen Mode

You must remain in full-screen mode throughout the exam. Exiting full-screen will be treated as a violation.

AI Face & Voice Monitoring

Your face and microphone are continuously monitored using on-device AI while the exam is in progress.

Face & Voice Calibration

Before the exam, you must complete a short face-calibration step, followed by a brief microphone calibration where you must stay silent. The exam cannot proceed until both steps are complete.

Continuous Presence

Your face must remain visible to the camera at all times. If your face leaves the frame for more than 5 seconds, a violation will be recorded.

Two Chances for Face Issues

If your face is not visible, or if a second person / an extra face appears in the camera frame, this counts as a face violation. You are allowed 2 such violations — on the 2nd one, the exam is terminated immediately.

Zero-Tolerance Voice Policy

Your microphone is monitored continuously. The system is calibrated to your room's background noise before the exam starts, so it can detect even a soft voice. If any speech or voice is detected at any point, the exam ends immediately — there are no extra chances for this rule. Whatever you have answered so far will be submitted automatically.

Browser & Window Restrictions

Switching tabs, minimizing the browser, losing window focus, exiting full-screen, right-clicking, copying/pasting, or attempting to open developer tools will each be recorded as a violation.

Violation Policy

A maximum of 5 general violations (tab switching, focus loss, full-screen exit, blocked shortcuts, etc.) are permitted. Exceeding this limit will auto-terminate the exam. Face and voice violations follow their own separate, stricter rules described above.

Time Limit

Each question has a maximum of 60 seconds. When the timer for a question expires, the system automatically moves to the next question. The exam auto-submits after the final question.

Academic Integrity

You must complete the exam independently. No external assistance or materials are allowed.

No Retake Policy

This examination allows one attempt only. There is no retake under any circumstances, including failure, cheating/violation termination, browser closure, page refresh, feedback submission, or leaving the examination.

Data & Notifications

Your name, email, course, exam data, and feedback will be sent to the Examination Administration.

Single Active Session

This exam can only run in one browser tab/window at a time. Opening it elsewhere will lock the duplicate session.

Screen & Identity Watermark

A faint, traceable watermark containing your name and a session ID is overlaid on the exam screen for the duration of the test.

Automation & DevTools Detection

The system detects automated browsers and attempts to open developer tools. Such attempts are logged as violations.

Final Decision

All activities are recorded. The examination authority reserves the right to review logs and make final decisions.

Your data will be sent to Administration. This includes your identity, exam result, and feedback.
